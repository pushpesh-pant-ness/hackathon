Autonomous Post-Deployment Incident & Remediation Agent
Business Scenario
Post-deployment incidents require engineering teams to continuously monitor alerts, investigate logs, identify root causes and determine corrective actions.
The submitted problem statement proposes an intelligent AI agent that can autonomously monitor, prioritize and resolve post-deployment issues while reducing manual effort and improving system reliability.
Capstone Objective
Develop an AI-powered operational agent capable of:
•	Monitoring application health
•	Analysing incidents
•	Identifying probable root causes
•	Searching historical incidents
•	Recommending remediation
•	Obtaining human approval
•	Executing approved actions
•	Validating the result
